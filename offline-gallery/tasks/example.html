Work item · illustrative sketch

Task brief

A compact place to agree on the outcome, capture observable checks, and hand off a bounded piece of work.

Owner: teamStatus: ready for reviewScope: one work item

Outcome

Make the next step clear enough that someone else can pick it up without reconstructing the whole conversation.

Acceptance evidence

  • The intended result is visible in the finished artifact.
  • A repeatable check demonstrates the normal path.
  • A limitation or failure path is recorded honestly.

Handoff

Summarize what changed, where the evidence lives, and what remains open. Leave decisions for the next owner explicit.
